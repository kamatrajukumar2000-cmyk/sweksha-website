CVD Diamond Machine Gas Line & Gas Purity Problems

Gas delivery and purity are critical to stable CVD diamond growth. Incorrect connections, leaks, contamination, regulator issues or supply instability can affect process repeatability. Diagnose the system using the machine manufacturer's approved procedure and qualified personnel.

Common symptoms

  • Unexpected gas-flow readings or pressure fluctuations
  • Unstable plasma or inconsistent process results
  • Suspected contamination, leak, regulator or valve issue
  • Unexpected alarms after a gas-cylinder or line change

Safe checks

  • Confirm the correct gas identity, cylinder label and approved supply specification
  • Review regulator, line and fitting inspection records
  • Compare flow and pressure readings with approved operating documentation
  • Use suitable leak-detection procedures and qualified technicians

Important safety note

Do not loosen pressurised fittings, improvise gas connections, or use unapproved leak-testing materials. Follow site gas-handling procedures and the machine manufacturer's instructions.

Need technical support?

Share your machine model, the alarm or symptom, pressure/flow readings where available, and recent maintenance history. Do not send passwords or confidential access credentials.
