3. Typical Functional Features to Confirm

  • Laser source: type, manufacturer, model, wavelength, rated output and operating hours.
  • Optical delivery: beam path, focusing lens, protective window, alignment method and optical maintenance requirements.
  • Motion and positioning: installed X/Y/Z or rotary axes, travel range, positioning accuracy, repeatability and fixture arrangement.
  • Computer control: installed control software, job file format, parameter library, operator permissions, alarms and data backup.
  • Diamond workholding: clamps, fixtures, rotary holder or other mounting method, plus supported stone dimensions and shapes.
  • Process capability: supported cutting, sawing, grooving or contour operations, material limits and achievable kerf/finish—confirm by sample test.
  • Safety system: enclosure rating, door interlocks, emergency stop, warning lamps, key control and approved laser eyewear requirements.
  • Utilities: electrical input, earthing, cooling/chiller needs, compressed air, extraction and environmental limits, as specified for the installed configuration.
  • Service access: replaceable optics, filters, cooling components, alignment points, maintenance intervals and available spare parts.

The items above are a technical verification checklist. They are not claims that every feature is present on the pictured machine.

4. General Operating Workflow

  1. Verify the machine identity, laser safety classification, nameplate details and operator manual.
  2. Inspect the enclosure, door interlocks, emergency stop, warning indicators and extraction/cooling arrangements.
  3. Confirm that the diamond fixture and workholding method are suitable for the intended job.
  4. Prepare the job using the machine's approved software and validated process settings.
  5. Check focus, alignment and workpiece position using the manufacturer's approved procedure.
  6. Run the process only with trained personnel, guards closed and safety systems functioning.
  7. Inspect the processed stone and record the job settings, consumables and results.
  8. Shut down, isolate and clean the machine according to the manufacturer instructions.

5. Installation & Utility Checklist

  • Stable, level floor with adequate service clearance around the cabinet.
  • Correct electrical supply, protective earthing and circuit protection.
  • Cooling/chiller connection and coolant specification, if required by the installed laser source.
  • Air supply, extraction or filtration, if specified by the manufacturer.
  • Ambient temperature, humidity, dust and vibration within the machine's rated limits.
  • Laser-controlled area signage, access control and required personal protective equipment.
  • Backup of software, configuration files and machine parameter records.

6. Maintenance & Troubleshooting

Laser output / cutting quality changes

Stop production and have a qualified technician inspect the laser source status, optics cleanliness, focus, alignment, fixture stability and validated process settings.

Positioning or dimensional errors

Check workpiece clamping, fixture condition, axis alarms, calibration records, backlash and motion-system condition using approved procedures.

Overheating or temperature alarms

Check the specified cooling circuit, coolant level and quality, flow, filters, heat exchanger and ambient conditions. Do not bypass temperature protection.

Computer or software fault

Record error messages, verify approved power and connections, preserve configuration backups and use manufacturer-approved recovery steps.

Door / interlock warning

Stop operation. Inspect door closure and interlock status; never bridge or disable safety switches.

Ventilation / extraction issue

Inspect filters, ducts, airflow and ventilation openings according to the maintenance schedule.

Maintain a service log for operating hours, alarms, cleaning, optics inspection, coolant changes, calibration, repairs and replacement parts.

7. Laser Safety

  • Operate only with trained and authorized personnel.
  • Never operate with the enclosure open or safety interlocks defeated.
  • Do not look into the beam path or optical apertures.
  • Use wavelength- and power-appropriate laser protective eyewear where the risk assessment and manufacturer require it.
  • Keep the processing enclosure, warning indicators and emergency stop in serviceable condition.
  • Follow applicable laser safety standards and the manufacturer's documented procedures.
  • Isolate electrical and laser energy before maintenance; service the laser source only by qualified personnel.
