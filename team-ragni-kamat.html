09 / HOSTING & SECURITYDomain, Hosting, Backup & Website SecurityKeep websites maintainable and protected

Hosting and security work depends on the platform, provider and access level. The team can plan and coordinate configuration without promising that any system is risk-free.

  • Domain/DNS, SSL certificate and hosting configuration review
  • Environment variables and secret-key handling; never publish credentials in source code
  • Backup schedule, restore testing and change history
  • Role-based access, account recovery and least-privilege review
  • Dependency updates, basic security headers and vulnerability follow-up
  • Uptime, error logs and performance monitoring setup

As Head of Digital Technology, IT & Innovation, Ragni Kamat leads SWEKSHA's unified digital function. Software development, website management, IT support and online growth are treated as connected responsibilities within one department.

The department supports SWEKSHA's own digital platforms and can also undertake external consultancy and project work for clients, subject to requirements, capacity and agreed scope.

Consultancy for India & international clients

SWEKSHA's digital department can discuss consultancy and project requirements from clients across India and internationally. The engagement may cover website development, software requirements, IT systems, technical support, SEO or digital marketing. Deliverables, timelines, pricing, access requirements and support arrangements are confirmed individually before work begins.

Any third-party platform, hosting, software licence, advertising spend or external service is subject to the relevant provider's terms and separate approval where required.

How a digital project moves forward

  • Understand: discuss the business goal, audience, existing systems and expected outcome.
  • Define: agree the project scope, features, responsibilities and deliverables.
  • Plan: confirm timeline, resources, access requirements and commercial terms.
  • Build or support: carry out the agreed design, development, IT or marketing work.
  • Review: test the result, collect feedback and agree the next improvements or support plan.
