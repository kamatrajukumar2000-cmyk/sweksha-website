Understand Your CVD / MPCVD Machine Systems

Explore a simplified visual overview of the major systems found in many CVD / MPCVD diamond-growth platforms. Learn what each system does, what symptoms may indicate a problem, and what information to collect before requesting technical support.

Animated system overview

The moving paths represent conceptual gas flow, vacuum connection and power input. They are not live measurements or operating instructions.

How to read it: green dashed paths indicate conceptual gas/cooling connections, blue paths indicate vacuum-related connections, and the gold path indicates a conceptual power input. Actual equipment may route these systems differently.

Explore the main machine systems

1. Vacuum system

Chamber, vacuum pump, valves, gauges and leak integrity. Record pressure readings, pump status and when the issue occurs.

2. Gas delivery

Gas supply, regulators, tubing, valves and mass-flow controllers. Record setpoints, displayed readings and alarms; never change gas settings without an approved procedure.

3. Microwave / RF power

Power source, transmission path and matching components vary by platform. High voltage and microwave energy require trained, authorized personnel.

4. Plasma and chamber

Plasma behaviour depends on the complete process and machine configuration. Document visual changes and alarms without opening or bypassing safety systems.

5. Cooling system

Cooling circuits remove heat from selected components. Note flow/temperature alarms and visible leaks; follow the manufacturer's shutdown procedure.

6. PLC / HMI and sensors

Controls coordinate interlocks, monitoring and sequences. Capture exact alarm text and sensor readings before requesting diagnosis.
