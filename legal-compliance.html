DEPARTMENT RESPONSIBILITIES

Structured Legal Support for Business Operations

The department is intended to help SWEKSHA identify legal questions early, organise records, coordinate with the right specialists and track actions. Formal legal opinions, drafting where reserved, court appearances and representation must be handled by an advocate or other professional legally authorised for the relevant work.

01 / CONTRACTS

Agreements & Contract Coordination

  • Customer, supplier and vendor agreements
  • Purchase orders, quotations and service terms
  • Payment milestones, delivery and warranty clauses
  • Confidentiality and non-disclosure arrangements
  • Renewal dates, obligations and document records
02 / DISPUTES

Legal Issues & Dispute Coordination

  • Collect facts, timelines and supporting documents
  • Organise correspondence, invoices and evidence
  • Coordinate advocate review of notices and replies
  • Track deadlines, meetings and agreed next steps
  • Support negotiation or settlement coordination through counsel
03 / TRADE

Import, Export & Trade Compliance

  • Coordinate checks for product import eligibility
  • Review applicable licences, permits and restrictions
  • Coordinate HS classification and customs questions with specialists
  • Check trade terms, shipping documents and contractual responsibilities
  • Flag country-specific regulatory requirements for professional review
04 / CORPORATE

Corporate & Business Documentation

  • Company records, resolutions and authorisations
  • Business policies and internal procedures
  • Employment, consultant and contractor documentation
  • Vendor onboarding and customer terms
  • Record retention and document version control
05 / PROTECTION

Intellectual Property & Confidentiality

  • Trademark, brand and domain-name issue coordination
  • Copyright and permitted-use awareness
  • Confidential business and technical information handling
  • Ownership and licence terms for software and creative work
  • Referral to IP specialists where needed
06 / PREVENTION

Risk Management & Compliance Calendar

  • Identify recurring legal and operational risk areas
  • Maintain a register of renewals and important dates
  • Coordinate tax, labour, safety and industry compliance queries
  • Escalate high-risk matters for professional assessment
  • Document actions, approvals and closure status
WORKFLOW

How a Legal Concern Will Be Handled

A clear, documented path so that issues are not missed and specialist help can be engaged at the right time.

STEP 01

Receive

Record the concern, parties involved, location, dates and any urgent deadline.

STEP 02

Organise

Collect contracts, invoices, messages, notices and supporting records securely.

STEP 03

Assess & Refer

Identify the subject area and coordinate review by an appropriately qualified advocate or specialist.

STEP 04

Track

Record professional guidance, approved actions, deadlines and follow-up status.

Areas Covered Across SWEKSHA

The legal function can coordinate matters arising from the organisation's business activities, including:

  • CVD and MPCVD equipment procurement, service and maintenance contracts
  • International sourcing, import-export and supplier transactions
  • Medical equipment sourcing, subject to applicable regulatory controls
  • Textile machinery purchase, sale and service arrangements
  • Customer, employee, consultant and vendor relationships
  • Website, digital content, branding and intellectual property matters

What Information to Keep Ready

For faster review, keep the relevant documents together:

  • Names and contact details of parties involved
  • Signed contract, quotation, invoice or purchase order
  • Chronology of events and important dates
  • Email, WhatsApp or other written communication
  • Payment proof, shipping papers or delivery records
  • Any legal notice, case reference or official communication

Do not send passwords, OTPs, banking PINs or unnecessary sensitive personal information through an enquiry form.

SWEKSHA PROFESSIONAL SERVICES · CLIENT CONSULTANCY

Legal & Compliance Consultancy Services

SWEKSHA intends to make organised legal and compliance support available not only for its own business operations, but also as a consultancy-coordination service for businesses, manufacturers, traders, importers, exporters and service providers. Each assignment is scoped transparently. Specialist legal advice and representation are provided only by an independently engaged, appropriately qualified advocate or authorised professional.

CONSULTANCY 01

Business Legal Readiness

Identify documents and professional reviews commonly needed before starting supplier, customer, service or distribution relationships.

  • Business-document checklist and gap identification
  • Vendor/customer onboarding documentation coordination
  • Contract workflow and approval checklist
  • Referral for legal drafting or formal legal opinion
CONSULTANCY 02

Contract & Commercial Coordination

Organise commercial terms and coordinate review of proposed agreements with the client's appointed advocate.

  • Purchase, sale, supply and service agreement coordination
  • Delivery, installation, acceptance and warranty schedules
  • Payment terms, milestones and responsibility matrix
  • NDA, confidentiality and IP clauses for specialist review
CONSULTANCY 03

Import–Export Compliance Coordination

Prepare transaction checklists and identify questions needing customs, tax, DGFT or legal specialist confirmation.

  • Product and transaction information checklist
  • HS classification query coordination with customs experts
  • IEC, invoice, packing list and shipping-document readiness
  • Restricted goods, permits, standards and country-specific checks
  • Incoterms, insurance and delivery-responsibility review
CONSULTANCY 04

Regulatory & Compliance Calendar

Organise recurring obligations and evidence of completion.

  • Compliance-obligation register and due-date tracker
  • Licence, registration and renewal document coordination
  • Employment, workplace and vendor-policy checklist coordination
  • Escalation of tax, labour, safety and sector-specific questions
CONSULTANCY 05

Dispute Documentation Support

Assemble a clear factual record before the client meets their advocate. SWEKSHA does not independently represent clients or give legal opinions.

  • Chronology and event summary preparation
  • Invoice, order, delivery and communication index
  • Notice/reply document collection for advocate review
  • Deadline and follow-up coordination
CONSULTANCY 06

Industry-Specific Coordination

Practical document coordination for equipment, manufacturing, trading and service businesses.

  • CVD/MPCVD equipment purchase, installation and service terms
  • Medical-equipment sourcing and regulatory referral checks
  • Textile machinery purchase, supply and maintenance contracts
  • Industrial machinery warranty, acceptance and spare-parts terms
CLIENT ENGAGEMENT JOURNEY

From First Discussion to Clear Next Steps

Every consultancy request follows a documented, scope-first process. No fixed outcome or approval is promised.

01 · DISCOVERY

Understand

Understand the business, transaction, location, industry, issue and desired support.

02 · SCOPE

Define

Confirm deliverables, exclusions, timeline, records and whether specialist counsel is needed.

03 · COORDINATE

Prepare

Organise checklists and commercial information; coordinate specialist review where required.

04 · DELIVER

Close & Track

Share agreed outputs, open points, responsible parties and follow-up dates.

Typical Consultancy Deliverables

  • Initial requirement and issue summary
  • Document checklist and missing-information register
  • Commercial and transaction workflow checklist
  • Compliance calendar or action tracker, where agreed
  • Questions list for the client's advocate, CA, customs broker or sector expert
  • Meeting notes and follow-up action register

Transparent Engagement & Fees

  • Scope and responsibilities confirmed before work begins
  • Fees quoted according to complexity, documents, time and specialist involvement
  • Government charges, filing fees, duties and third-party professional fees are separate unless expressly included in writing
  • No guaranteed court result, licence, customs clearance, tax outcome or regulatory approval
  • Confidential records handled through an agreed secure process

A written engagement note should identify the service provider, deliverables, fees, exclusions, timelines and applicable professional terms.

Qualified Legal Advice & Representation

This page describes SWEKSHA's planned legal coordination function and is general business information, not legal advice.
