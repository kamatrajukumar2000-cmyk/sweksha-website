How this enquiry system works

  • Choose whether you want to buy or sell.
  • Share the part name, machine make/model, quantity, photos and location.
  • Sweksha checks the enquiry details and may help identify a suitable contact.
  • Buyer and seller confirm price, condition, payment, shipping and warranty directly in writing.

Please note: This is an enquiry and sourcing service, not a live inventory or escrow marketplace. A listing or enquiry does not mean an item is in stock, verified, or guaranteed. Do not send advance payment until you have independently checked the seller, item, condition and transaction terms. For used machines, request inspection, operating video, serial/nameplate details and service history where available.
