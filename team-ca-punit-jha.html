Relevant responsibilities include planning and coordinating audit procedures, reviewing supporting records, assessing risk areas and communicating observations for appropriate resolution.

  • Review of financial records and supporting evidence
  • Risk assessment and control evaluation
  • Identification of potential misstatements and control gaps

Taxation & regulatory awareness

Tax work requires accurate records, disciplined timelines and careful review of applicable requirements. Punit's professional focus includes taxation matters and identifying potential tax exposures that may require attention from the organisation and its appointed advisers.

Financial reporting
