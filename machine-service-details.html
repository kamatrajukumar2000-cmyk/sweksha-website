CVD / MPCVD Machine Repair & Troubleshooting

Our technical support covers common machine systems and operating faults, including:

  • Vacuum system, vacuum pump, chamber leakage and helium leak testing
  • Gas line, SS tubing, VCR fittings, gas supply and MFC flow errors
  • Pressure controller, Pirani gauge, throttle valve and pressure stability
  • Microwave / RF power supply, magnetron and power-related faults
  • Plasma ignition failure, plasma instability and process interruptions
  • Electrical panels, PLC, HMI, sensors and control-system faults
  • Cooling system, water flow, temperature and mechanical issues
  • Machine installation, commissioning, inspection and technical consultation
