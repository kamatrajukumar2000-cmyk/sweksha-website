Helium Leak Testing & Vacuum Pressure Monitoring

Helium Leak Testing for CVD Diamond Machines

Helium leak testing is an important maintenance procedure for checking the vacuum integrity of CVD diamond reactors. It helps identify leakage in vacuum chambers, flange connections, O-rings, valves, feedthroughs, and vacuum piping. A properly maintained vacuum system helps the CVD diamond machine maintain stable chamber pressure and controlled process gas conditions during production.

A reactor may require a different leak-rate limit based on its design, pumping capacity, and process requirements.

2. Vacuum Pressure and Helium Leak Testing

During a helium leak test, the vacuum chamber or selected test section is evacuated using the appropriate pumping system. The technician checks the pressure reading and verifies that the vacuum system is suitable for the leak detector's operating range.

Helium is then applied near suspected leakage points. If helium enters through a leak path, the detector measures the tracer gas response and helps locate the leakage. A vacuum pressure reading alone cannot confirm that the system is leak-free. Pressure rise may also result from outgassing, moisture, virtual leaks, pump performance problems, or valve issues.

3. Common Vacuum Problems and Diagnostic Checks

Chamber Pressure Is Higher Than Expected

Possible causes: leakage through chamber seals or fittings, poor vacuum pump performance, contamination or outgassing, incorrect valve position, or vacuum gauge measurement error.

Recommended checks: inspect chamber sealing surfaces and connections; check vacuum pump condition; perform a suitable leak test; verify gauge calibration and valve operation.

Slow Vacuum Pump-Down

Possible causes: vacuum leakage, moisture or contamination inside the chamber, pumping line restrictions, worn or inefficient vacuum pump, or incorrect pumping configuration.

Recommended checks: compare pump-down behavior with the machine's normal baseline; inspect vacuum lines, valves and chamber connections; investigate possible leakage using an appropriate method; check pump maintenance condition.

Pressure Instability During CVD Operation

Possible causes: vacuum leakage, unstable gas flow, throttle valve control problems, pressure sensor errors, or vacuum pump performance changes.

Recommended checks: review pressure sensor readings; inspect throttle valve response and controller output; verify gas-flow stability; check pump performance; confirm suspected leakage using an approved test procedure.

4. Helium Leak Testing Procedure

  1. Shut down the CVD process and place the equipment in a safe condition.
  2. Inspect the chamber, flanges, seals, valves, and vacuum connections.
  3. Connect the helium leak detector according to the manufacturer's instructions.
  4. Evacuate the test section to the required test condition.
  5. Verify detector calibration and background signal.
  6. Apply helium in a controlled manner near suspected leakage points.
  7. Identify and evaluate the detector response against the specified acceptance limit.
  8. Repair defective seals or connections using approved components.
  9. Repeat the test and document the final result.
Safety: Testing must follow the reactor and leak-detector manuals. Do not test around an operating plasma, energized microwave/RF or high-voltage equipment, hot chamber parts, or hazardous process-gas systems unless an approved procedure specifically permits it. Do not loosen pressurized gas fittings to investigate a leak.

5. How Helium Leak Testing Supports Diamond Production

Vacuum leakage can introduce unwanted atmospheric gases into the CVD reactor. Depending on the leakage level and process conditions, this may disturb chamber pressure, gas composition, and plasma stability. Helium leak testing helps identify leakage that may contribute to these problems.

Diamond growth quality also depends on substrate preparation, gas purity, process pressure, temperature, microwave/RF power, and process duration. Passing a helium leak test does not by itself guarantee diamond quality or production capacity.
