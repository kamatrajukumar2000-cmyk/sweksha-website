CVD Diamond Machine Plasma Instability

Plasma instability can reflect changing process load. Pressure, gas flow, power coupling, chamber condition, temperature and geometry can interact. Compare trends rather than changing multiple settings at once.

Common symptoms

  • Plasma flickers, shifts or changes shape
  • Plasma extinguishes and reignites
  • Pressure, power or flow readings fluctuate

Possible causes

  • Pressure-control or gas-flow instability
  • Microwave/RF coupling or reflected-power changes
  • Chamber deposits, cooling, temperature or sensor issue

Safe first checks

  • Log pressure, flow, power and alarms together
  • Compare with approved recipe and stable runs
  • Check whether it follows a process stage or maintenance event
Safety: Do not defeat interlocks or make uncontrolled power, pressure or gas changes. Follow machine procedures and trained-personnel requirements.
