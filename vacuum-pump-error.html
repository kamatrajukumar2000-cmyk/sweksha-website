CVD Diamond Machine Vacuum Pump Error

The vacuum system removes gas and helps maintain process pressure. Pump performance depends on pump condition, valves, seals, exhaust path, gas load and pressure-control systems.

Common symptoms

  • Pump-down takes longer than normal
  • Pressure rises or fluctuates
  • Temperature, noise, vibration or pump alarm changes

Possible causes

  • Leaks or degraded seals
  • Oil/service condition on oil-sealed pumps
  • Blocked exhaust, contamination, gauge or control issue

Safe first checks

  • Record pump-down time, pressure trend and alarms
  • Check approved operator-level indicators and service records
  • Arrange qualified leak testing, gauge verification and pump inspection
Safety: Do not disconnect vacuum lines unsafely or open pump/electrical assemblies without isolation and trained authorization.
