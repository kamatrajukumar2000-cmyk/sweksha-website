Throttle Valve & Pressure Stability Problems

Stable chamber pressure depends on the vacuum system, throttle valve, pressure measurement, gas flow and control settings working together. A pressure fluctuation should be diagnosed from trends and alarms rather than by changing settings at random.

Common symptoms

  • Chamber pressure hunts or oscillates
  • Pressure does not reach the setpoint
  • Slow response after gas-flow or recipe changes
  • Throttle valve position or pressure sensor readings appear inconsistent

Useful diagnostic information

  • Record target and actual pressure over time
  • Note throttle-valve position, gas-flow readings and alarm codes
  • Check maintenance history and inspect for obvious leaks using approved procedures
  • Compare sensor readings and valve response with the manufacturer's service procedure

Important safety note

Do not bypass interlocks or adjust vacuum-control parameters without authorization. Follow the equipment manufacturer's procedures; work on electrical, RF/microwave and vacuum systems should be performed by qualified personnel.

Need technical support?

Share your machine model, the alarm or symptom, pressure/flow readings where available, and recent maintenance history. Do not send passwords or confidential access credentials.
