CVD Machine Defect Knowledge Base

Find the Machine Defect & Troubleshoot the Cause.

Search and filter common CVD diamond manufacturing machine problems across vacuum, gas, microwave/RF, plasma, cooling, electrical and sensor systems.

🌀

Vacuum Leak

Pressure does not reach the expected base pressure or rises after isolation. Check seals, flanges, valves, feedthroughs and chamber condition.

Vacuum
🔧

Vacuum Pump Problem

Slow pump-down, poor pumping capacity, contamination or abnormal temperature. Inspect pump condition, oil, exhaust and valve configuration.

Vacuum
💨

MFC Flow Error

Displayed and actual gas flow may differ because of calibration drift, valve faults, contamination or control/communication problems.

Gas
🧪

Gas Line / Purity Problem

Leakage, blockage, moisture or contamination can change plasma chemistry and diamond growth. Check regulators, fittings, purging and purification.

Gas
⚡

Microwave / RF Power Fault

Unstable forward power, high reflected power, poor tuning or source instability can prevent stable plasma operation.

Microwave / RF
🪟

Quartz Window Problem

Carbon deposition, cracking or thermal damage can disturb microwave coupling and change plasma behavior.

Microwave / RF
🔥

Plasma Ignition Failure

Plasma does not ignite, ignites and disappears, or requires abnormal conditions. Check pressure, gas, power, tuning and chamber condition.

Plasma
☄️

Plasma Instability

Flickering, movement, oscillation or sudden extinction may indicate pressure, flow, power, geometry, temperature or contamination problems.

Plasma
❄️

Cooling Failure

Low coolant flow, high temperature, pump failure or heat-exchanger restriction can cause overheating and machine trips.

Cooling
🔌

Electrical / PLC Fault

Power instability, relay failure, PLC I/O faults, HMI communication problems and interlock errors can stop the machine.

Electrical
📡

Sensor / Measurement Error

Incorrect calibration, drift, signal noise, open circuits or communication failure can create false process readings.

Sensors
🧱

Chamber Carbon Deposition

Deposits can alter plasma behavior, create particles and reduce process repeatability. Inspect chamber, holder and quartz surfaces.

Chamber
Diamond Growth Defects

Common CVD Diamond Quality Problems.

Use the machine and process checks together when investigating growth quality.

🧊

Non-Uniform Thickness

Investigate temperature distribution, plasma position, gas distribution, substrate placement and chamber geometry.

〰️

Surface Roughness

Review nucleation, temperature, growth conditions, process stability and substrate preparation.

⚫

Non-Diamond Carbon

Check process chemistry, contamination, chamber condition and plasma stability when undesirable carbon appears.

🕳️

Pinholes / Voids

Look for particles, poor nucleation, surface contamination and non-uniform growth.

💥

Cracking

Investigate residual stress, thermal gradients, thickness, thermal cycling and substrate mismatch.

↗️

Delamination

Check adhesion, substrate preparation, stress and thermal expansion effects.

🔬

Inclusions

Possible sources include particles, chamber deposits, substrate material and process contamination.

🌡️

Temperature-Related Defects

Temperature gradients can affect growth rate, morphology, stress and uniformity.

🧬

Impurity / Defect Variation

Gas purity, leaks and process chemistry can influence impurity-related optical and material properties.

Diagnostic rule: Do not change many process parameters at the same time. Record pressure, gas flow, temperature, forward/reflected power and relevant alarms first.
Safety: Microwave/RF power, high voltage, vacuum, pressurized gases and hot chambers can be hazardous. Only trained personnel should service equipment. Never bypass safety interlocks.
