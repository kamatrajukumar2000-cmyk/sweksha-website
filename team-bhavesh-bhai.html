Department leadership

Mr. Bhavesh Bhai leads the international sourcing and trade-coordination function within SWEKSHA's Core Department, connecting overseas supplier options with the requirements of Indian businesses.

Supplier and product verification

Available supplier information, model details, specifications, warranty terms and supporting evidence are organised for buyer review. Independent inspection, certification or legal verification is arranged with appropriate specialists where required.

Customer communication

Requirements, quotations, questions and next steps are coordinated clearly so buyers understand what is confirmed, what remains subject to supplier response and what needs independent professional checking.

Professional coordination boundaries

Customs classification, statutory advice, regulated medical-device approvals, tax positions and legal opinions must be confirmed by the relevant authorised professionals. No supplier, product availability, approval or delivery outcome is guaranteed until verified.

How a sourcing enquiry moves forward

  • Understand: capture the product, model, application, quantity and destination.
  • Source: identify potential suppliers and request information.
  • Compare: review technical specifications, quotation, lead time and estimated landed cost.
  • Coordinate: connect the buyer with supplier, freight and customs professionals.
  • Follow up: track agreed documents, shipment milestones and delivery questions.
