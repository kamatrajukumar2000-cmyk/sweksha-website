Leadership vision

Mr Raju leads SWEKSHA with the aim of making practical CVD and MPCVD technical support easier to access for diamond-industry businesses. The operating vision is to build a service organisation that understands real plant conditions, listens carefully to machine owners and coordinates the right technical response instead of offering generic promises.

The focus is to grow from a strong Surat base towards service coverage across India, supported by a dedicated technical team and a clear customer-first operating approach.

Industry experience

Bringing around six years of experience across CVD and lab-grown diamond industry projects, Mr Raju's work has centred on understanding machine behaviour, production challenges and the relationship between equipment condition and growth outcomes.

His perspective connects machine-level troubleshooting with process stability, production requirements and practical business decisions. Specific service scope is reviewed against the machine, site conditions and available technical information.

CVD machine service focus

  • Machine installation, setup, commissioning and revival planning
  • Vacuum system checks, pump-down behaviour and leak investigation
  • Gas delivery, gas-line integrity, MFC and pressure-control issues
  • Plasma stability, microwave/RF and power-system coordination
  • Cooling, sensors, electrical panels, PLC/HMI and control interfaces
  • Preventive maintenance and structured fault diagnosis

Growth process & production

Growth performance is influenced by multiple interacting variables. Mr Raju's process-development focus includes reviewing the machine condition and process information together, understanding reported growth-rate or quality concerns, and helping teams identify which variables need controlled investigation.

Recommendations are based on the specific process, equipment configuration and data shared by the customer. No universal recipe or guaranteed production outcome is promised.

How SWEKSHA approaches a customer requirement

  • Understand: capture machine type, model, symptoms, recent changes, operating conditions and production objective.
  • Review: assess the available photos, videos, readings, alarms and service history before defining next checks.
  • Plan: discuss the likely service scope, required resources, site readiness, availability and commercial terms.
  • Execute: coordinate online guidance or on-site technical work, subject to confirmed scope and availability.
  • Follow through: review the outcome with the customer and identify sensible preventive actions.

This structured approach is intended to reduce guesswork, improve communication and keep technical decisions connected to the customer's real operating needs.

Building a dependable service team

Mr Raju's leadership responsibility includes coordinating technicians across electrical and PLC/HMI systems, magnetron and power supplies, vacuum and gas lines, MFCs and pressure controllers, mechanical and cooling systems, installation and commissioning, and CVD growth-process support. Work is assigned according to the requirement and the team's available capability.

SWEKSHA's service model is designed for both remote support and travel-based site work across India, Monday to Saturday, subject to scheduling and agreement. Testing equipment and the exact resources required are confirmed before each engagement.
