CVD Diamond Machine MFC Flow Error

A mass flow controller regulates gas flow. Displayed flow may differ because of gas selection, pressure, calibration, valve/control faults or communication issues. Gas compatibility and MFC specifications matter.

Common symptoms

  • Setpoint and displayed flow disagree
  • Flow oscillates, stays at zero or reaches a limit
  • MFC alarm or communication error

Possible causes

  • Incorrect gas configuration or units
  • Supply pressure outside operating range
  • Contamination, restriction, calibration or communication fault

Safe first checks

  • Record gas identity, setpoint, displayed flow and alarms
  • Confirm gas and units against approved recipe and MFC label
  • Check external supply indicators; qualified personnel verify calibration
Safety: Do not loosen pressurized gas fittings, change recipes arbitrarily, or use an MFC for an incompatible gas. Follow purge and leak-check procedures.
