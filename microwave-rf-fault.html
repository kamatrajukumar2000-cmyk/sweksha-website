CVD Diamond Machine Microwave / RF Fault

Microwave or RF power sustains plasma in many CVD reactors. A fault may appear as failure to ignite, unstable output, high reflected power or repeated trips. Systems vary by source and design.

Common symptoms

  • No plasma ignition or unexpected extinction
  • Forward/reflected power readings change
  • Source, tuning, cooling or interlock alarms

Possible causes

  • Poor coupling or matching conditions
  • Cooling outside equipment limits
  • Source, transmission component or chamber-load fault

Safe first checks

  • Capture fault codes and power readings
  • Compare against approved recipe and baseline
  • Review cooling, pressure and gas status at operator level
Safety: Do not open energized high-voltage, microwave or RF assemblies, defeat interlocks, or adjust tuning blindly. Follow lockout and manufacturer procedures.
