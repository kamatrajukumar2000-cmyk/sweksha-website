How the Marketplace Works

  1. Buyer or seller submits machine details through this form.
  2. Submission is saved in the marketplace database as pending.
  3. Sweksha Admin reviews the model, details, photos and contact information.
  4. Approved listings become visible in the public machine marketplace.
  5. Interested buyers contact Sweksha to verify availability and arrange discussion or inspection.
Safety: Sweksha acts as an enquiry and connection point. Buyers should independently inspect the machine, verify ownership and confirm payment and delivery terms before purchasing.
