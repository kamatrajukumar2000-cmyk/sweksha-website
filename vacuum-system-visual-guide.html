CVD / MPCVD Vacuum System: Components & Concepts

A vacuum system removes gas from a chamber and helps establish and monitor the pressure conditions required by a machine's approved process. The exact pump arrangement, valves, gauges and control logic differ by machine model.

Illustration notice: The animated drawing below is a simplified educational concept. It is not a scale drawing, piping diagram, pump-sizing guide, operating sequence or representation of a specific manufacturer's machine.

Conceptual vacuum-line animation

Animated dashed lines show conceptual connections only. Real installations may include additional stages, traps, filters, backing pumps, isolation arrangements and interlocks.

What each component does

Process chamber

The sealed vessel where the process takes place. Seals, ports and windows are part of the chamber's integrity.

Vacuum pump system

Creates the required vacuum condition. Pump technology and configuration must be confirmed from the machine documentation.

Isolation valves

Control connections between sections of the vacuum system. Their position and interlocks are machine-specific.

Pressure gauges

Provide pressure readings within their designed measurement range. Gauge type, location and calibration matter.

Vacuum tubing & fittings

Connect components and must be compatible with the system's materials, seals and rated conditions.

Control & interlocks

Monitor system states and prevent unsafe or out-of-sequence operation according to the machine design.

When a vacuum-related issue appears

For a technician review, record the exact machine model, displayed pressure and units, gauge location, alarm wording, time trend, pump status and any recent maintenance or configuration changes. Photos of the HMI and external equipment labels can help.

Safety: Do not loosen fittings, open a chamber, disconnect vacuum lines, bypass interlocks or service energized equipment to investigate a fault. Follow the manufacturer's shutdown, venting and maintenance procedures and use trained, authorized personnel.
