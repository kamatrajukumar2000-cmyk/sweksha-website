Role within SWEKSHA

This position is intended to support SWEKSHA's own Core Department with structured records, contract coordination, compliance calendars and risk escalation.

Consultancy for external businesses

Potential clients include manufacturers, industrial units, importers, exporters, traders, start-ups and SMEs. Each engagement must have a written scope, deliverables, exclusions, timeline and fee arrangement.

Professional boundaries

Administrative, documentation and compliance-coordination support does not replace legal advice. Formal legal opinions, reserved legal drafting, litigation strategy and court representation must be handled by an appropriately qualified advocate or other professional authorised under applicable law.
