Before the first online class

  • Confirm the teacher's identity, qualifications and relevant teaching experience.
  • Agree on the class platform, lesson duration and communication channel.
  • Discuss fees, payment schedule, cancellation and rescheduling terms.
  • For minors, keep a parent or guardian involved in communication and class arrangements.
  • Arrange a trial or introductory session only if both sides agree.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you get started

Can students from different cities request online tuition?

Yes. The form accepts location and timing details. Suitable teacher availability and time-zone compatibility must still be confirmed.

Which classes can request online tuition?

Parents can submit requirements for LKG through Class 12. Mention the exact class, subject and syllabus.

Does an online tuition request guarantee a tutor?

No. A request is reviewed against teacher availability, subject fit and schedule. Any final arrangement is mutually confirmed.

What device is needed?

A suitable phone, tablet or computer with a stable internet connection is usually needed. Confirm the specific platform and materials with the teacher.

Teacher availability, qualifications, fees, timings and final teaching arrangements must be checked and mutually confirmed. A request does not guarantee a placement.
