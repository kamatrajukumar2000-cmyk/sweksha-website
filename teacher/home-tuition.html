HOME TUTOR REQUIREMENTS

What to include in a home tuition request

Clear details help us understand the requirement before discussing possible teacher options.

Class & Subjects

Share the student's class from LKG to Class 12, board or syllabus if relevant, and the subjects needing support.

City & Area

Provide the city, locality and a general nearby landmark. Do not post a full home address publicly.

Timing & Learning Goals

Tell us preferred days, time window, exam goals and areas where the student needs help.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you get started

Does Sweksha guarantee a home tutor in every area?

No. Availability depends on the city, locality, subject, schedule and teacher availability. Submit a request so the requirement can be reviewed.

Can I request tuition for LKG to Class 12?

Yes. The request form accepts classes from LKG through Class 12. Mention the exact subject and syllabus requirements.

Should I share my full home address in the form?

No. Share only your city and general locality initially. Confirm a precise address privately after appropriate checks and agreement.

Who decides the tuition fee?

The parent or guardian and teacher should mutually agree on fees, payment terms and schedule before classes start.

Teacher availability, qualifications, fees, timings and final teaching arrangements must be checked and mutually confirmed. A request does not guarantee a placement.
