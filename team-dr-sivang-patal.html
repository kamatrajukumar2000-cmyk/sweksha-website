Role overview

Dr. Sivang Patal's role at SWEKSHA spans two connected areas: diamond sales and trading, and CVD technical operations. The position brings customer communication, business development and service follow-through together so that commercial requirements and technical needs can be understood and routed clearly.

This profile describes the responsibilities assigned within SWEKSHA. It does not imply qualifications, certifications or years of experience that have not been independently provided.

Diamond sales & trading

Diamond business often involves multiple parties, product specifications and commercial expectations. The role includes helping buyers and sellers communicate clearly, understanding the requirement and supporting the progression of a potential transaction.

  • Understand buyer requirements and seller offerings
  • Coordinate product information and commercial discussions
  • Support negotiation and follow-up between parties
  • Build business relationships and identify relevant opportunities
  • Keep communication clear through the enquiry and deal process

Business development

Business development is built on listening, trust and consistent follow-through. The role includes connecting with diamond-industry participants, understanding their buying or selling needs and identifying where SWEKSHA's wider network or services may be relevant.

Any product description, availability, quality representation, pricing or transaction condition must be confirmed with the relevant buyer and seller before it is treated as final.

CVD technical support

On the technical side, the role includes customer-facing support for CVD/MPCVD machine requirements. This can involve receiving the issue description, collecting machine details and coordinating the right technical review or service response.

  • Machine installation, setup and commissioning coordination
  • Technical support and initial troubleshooting discussions
  • Maintenance requirements and service follow-through
  • Coordination with SWEKSHA technicians for specialist issues

From enquiry to service coordination

  • Listen to the requirement: capture the customer's objective, urgency and relevant background.
  • Collect useful details: for machine work, gather machine type/model, symptoms, readings, photos or videos where available.
  • Coordinate the right response: connect the requirement with the relevant SWEKSHA technical resource or business contact.
  • Clarify scope: align on expected work, availability, site conditions and commercial terms before proceeding.
  • Follow through: maintain communication so the customer knows the next step and the status of the enquiry.

Working with the SWEKSHA team

The role works alongside SWEKSHA's leadership and technical team. Diamond enquiries are handled through buyer–seller coordination, while machine requirements are directed to the appropriate technical capability across installation, commissioning, maintenance and troubleshooting. This joined-up approach helps customers reach the right point of contact without confusing commercial and technical responsibilities.

Connect with SWEKSHA

For diamond buying or selling enquiries, or for CVD/MPCVD machine installation and technical support, contact SWEKSHA and describe your requirement. The team will review the details and confirm the appropriate next step.
