CVD Diamond Machine Quartz Window Problem

The quartz window transmits microwave energy while separating the plasma chamber from the source. Deposits, damage or a compromised seal can affect coupling and stability. Window designs differ; use the equipment manufacturer's procedure.

Common symptoms

  • Carbon or film deposition visible
  • Cracks, chips, discoloration or unusual hot spots
  • Changed reflected-power behavior or unstable plasma

Possible causes

  • Process deposits accumulating
  • Thermal stress, poor cooling or uneven temperature
  • Mechanical damage or seal deterioration

Safe first checks

  • Record alarms and power trends before shutdown
  • Inspect only after approved isolation and cool-down
  • Have qualified personnel verify seals, cooling and coupling
Safety: Do not operate with a cracked window, bypass interlocks, or open microwave/RF enclosures. Microwave leakage checks and replacement require trained personnel.
