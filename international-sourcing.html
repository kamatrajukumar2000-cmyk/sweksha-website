COMPLETE IMPORT & EXPORT WORKFLOW

How International Purchasing Works — Step by Step

A practical guide for customers who want to source machinery, equipment or components from overseas suppliers and bring them into India, or explore export opportunities.

01 / REQUIREMENT

Share Your Requirement

Tell us exactly what you need so the sourcing search starts with the correct technical and commercial details.

  • Product name, brand, model and intended application
  • Technical datasheet, drawings, photos or reference links
  • Required quantity, new or refurbished condition
  • Target budget, preferred country and delivery city
  • Required delivery date and after-sales expectations
02 / SUPPLIER SEARCH

Find Suitable Overseas Suppliers

We can identify potential manufacturers, authorised distributors or exporters in relevant markets and request product information.

  • Shortlist suppliers against your specifications
  • Request company details and product catalogue
  • Ask for quotation, lead time and warranty terms
  • Clarify manufacturer, trader or agent status
03 / COMPARE QUOTATIONS

Compare Price & Terms

Compare quotations on a like-for-like basis rather than choosing only the lowest headline price.

  • Unit price, currency and quantity breaks
  • Included accessories, software and documentation
  • Payment milestones and production lead time
  • Warranty, installation and service conditions
  • Shipping terms such as EXW, FOB, CIF or DAP, as quoted
04 / TECHNICAL & SUPPLIER CHECK

Verify Before Ordering

Review available evidence before any purchase commitment. The level of verification depends on product type and supplier access.

  • Datasheets, serial/model details and configuration
  • Photos, live video demonstration or inspection report
  • Factory or supplier business credentials where available
  • Test reports, certificates and warranty documents
  • Reference checks and independent inspection if arranged
05 / IMPORT ELIGIBILITY

Check Indian Import Rules

Before payment, confirm that the exact product can legally be imported and used in India.

  • Correct product description and HS classification with a customs professional
  • Import policy, restricted/prohibited status and required licences
  • Applicable BIS, WPC, CDSCO or other approvals where relevant
  • Labelling, safety, technical and country-of-origin requirements
  • Importer of Record, IEC and GST readiness as applicable
06 / ORDER & PAYMENT

Approve the Purchase

Proceed only after the buyer accepts the final specification, total estimated cost, documents and supplier terms.

  • Review Proforma Invoice and purchase order
  • Confirm Incoterms, delivery point and risk transfer
  • Agree payment method and milestones in writing
  • Verify beneficiary bank details independently
  • Keep invoices, remittance records and correspondence
07 / SHIPPING

Arrange International Transport

Coordinate the shipment plan with the supplier and freight forwarder according to cargo size, urgency and product handling needs.

  • Air freight, sea freight or courier as suitable
  • Packing, crate dimensions, weight and insurance
  • Pickup, booking, shipping documents and tracking
  • Dangerous-goods or special-handling checks if applicable
  • Expected transit time and arrival port/airport
08 / CUSTOMS & TAXES

Customs Clearance in India

Coordinate with the appointed customs broker and importer for declarations, assessment, duties and release.

  • Commercial invoice and packing list
  • Bill of Lading, Air Waybill or courier documents
  • Certificate of Origin and insurance papers if required
  • Bill of Entry, duty assessment and payment
  • Inspection, examination or additional approvals if called for
09 / INDIA DELIVERY

Receive & Check the Goods

After customs release, coordinate onward transport and check the received goods against the approved order.

  • Local transport and delivery scheduling
  • Package condition and quantity check
  • Model, serial number and accessories verification
  • Record shortages or transit damage promptly
  • Installation, commissioning or service only where separately agreed
UNDERSTAND THE REAL COST

Estimate the Landed Cost Before You Commit

The overseas supplier price is not the final India purchase cost. We help organise an estimate of the complete landed cost so the buyer can compare suppliers and make an informed decision.

Landed cost estimate = Product price + origin charges + international freight + insurance + customs duty and applicable cess + import taxes + customs broker/port charges + local transport + other applicable expenses.

What the Estimate Should Show

  • Supplier quotation and currency conversion basis
  • Freight, insurance and origin handling charges
  • HS code and duty estimate confirmed by customs professional
  • IGST and other taxes as applicable
  • Port, terminal, clearance and broker charges
  • Transport to the final delivery location
  • Contingency for exchange-rate or freight changes

Any landed-cost figure is an estimate until the product classification, customs assessment, exchange rate, freight booking and actual charges are confirmed. Taxes may have different input-credit treatment; ask your accountant or tax adviser.
